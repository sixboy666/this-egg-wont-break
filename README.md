# THIS EGG WON'T BREAK

ONE TAP. ONE EGG. ZERO BRAKES. A vertical meme physics mini-game where a giant egg bounces downward through soft pads and absolutely refuses to break.

- 100% single-file HTML5, zero dependencies, zero loaded files - every sound is synthesized live with Web Audio
- One-button control: tap anywhere to flip the egg's bounce direction; it bounces on its own
- Six escalating chaos tiers: normal bounce, then it spins, goes sideways, dribbles like a basketball, grows a face, and finally starts actively DODGING you
- Cracks spread dynamically after every landing; at the limit the shell pops open to reveal a chick wearing sunglasses - who stares at you, then stuffs itself back inside. Fully healed. Run continues.
- Miss a pad and the egg becomes a pancake: "it is fine. YOU lost." No gore, pure entertainment
- Juice everywhere: squash & stretch, slow motion, camera zoom, shockwaves, particles and screen shake
- Generative music: each landing plays a different pentatonic pitch so bounces form a rhythm; tension rises with the cracks; the crack-open goes dead silent before one absurd sound
- 5 switchable scenes with original palettes: MORNING YOLK, COTTON CANDY, BLUEBERRY NIGHT, MATCHA ZEN, NEON PARTY
- Phone (tap) and desktop (mouse / space / enter); best score saved locally

## Play
- Online: https://sixboy666.github.io/this-egg-wont-break/
- Offline: download this-egg-wont-break.zip from Releases, unzip, open index.html
